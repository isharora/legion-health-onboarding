Demo scope

The example patient has selected anxiety only. The post-booking web view includes medical and treatment history, medication and allergy details, emergency contact, recent vital signs, photo ID, optional supporting documents and care-coordination permission, and the GAD-7. Required answers, an ID, and a digital signature drive the completion status. Changing saved information requires signing again.

Prototype controls and prefilled scenarios are presentation aids. Records-found scenarios include example diagnosis and treatment documents and assume a photo ID is already on file; they do not imply a records API supplies identity documents. Optional release permission still requires a patient choice. Provider availability, insurance lookup, records retrieval, messaging, uploads and signatures are simulated; no external clinical systems are connected.
